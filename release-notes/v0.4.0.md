More Panther Lake controls, live readouts and automatic launch options. **Not yet tested on a real device.**

## New in Quick Access

- **CPU speed cap** (30-100 %): leaves more of the power budget to the GPU.
- **CPU vs GPU balance** (1-5): the SoC's own power-sharing hint; works alongside power-profiles-daemon.
- **GPU power saving**: the GPU ramps up more slowly to save battery with a frame cap or in light games.
- **CPU minimum speed** (on the charger only), and **per-core-type caps** under Advanced.
- **Scheduler mode** on CachyOS (Auto / Gaming / Power save, through scx-loader), and **pause intel_lpmd while a game runs** when that service is active.
- **TDP check interval**: how often OXP3 Deck re-checks and re-applies the power settings (Off, 2-60 s).
- **Kernel 7.4 and later only** (hidden before): battery charge limit, "run from the charger, battery idle", and fan mode Auto / Quiet / Max with automatic fallback to Auto at 90 °C, on errors and before sleep.

## Also

- **Live readouts** while a panel is open: package watts, battery draw and time left, temperature, GPU busy % and clocks, and why it is slowing down.
- **Automatic launch options** (off by default): adds the variables a game needs (for example XeSS with xess-xmx-linux) without touching your own text, never while the game runs, with Undo. **Copy launch options** for when they cannot be set automatically: paste in the game's Properties > General > Launch options.
- **When it takes effect**: every setting is tagged Now, Restart the game, Log out of Game Mode or reboot, Reboot, or Only while no game runs, and shows what is still pending after a change.

Two values still need measuring on the device: the Quiet fan speed (about 40 %) and which end of the CPU vs GPU balance favours which.
