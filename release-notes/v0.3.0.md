Easier settings and "Fix for me". **Not yet tested on a real device.**

- **Shorter screens:** every control has a short name and one line saying what it does. The details (when it takes effect, risks, how to fix, technical names) are behind the ⓘ: press the ☰ (Options) button on a control, or A on a status row, to open them.
- **Fix for me:** when another service blocks a setting (power-profiles-daemon or TuneD blocking energy preference and CPU boost, thermald or intel_lpmd changing the power limits, PowerStation), its row says what is blocking and offers **Fix for me**. After you confirm a dialog that says exactly what will change, OXP3 Deck stops and disables that service (it never masks or uninstalls anything) and records it; **Undo** turns back on exactly what it turned off. A few services can start again when another program asks for them (for example KDE's power applet starts power-profiles-daemon): the row then says so and offers Fix again.
- Things that cannot be fixed safely from the plugin (another Decky plugin, Handheld Daemon, Steam's own sliders, a firmware-locked limit) get short step-by-step guidance instead.
