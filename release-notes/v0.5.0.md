Performance: one-tap power presets, a device check, low-battery saving, power tips while playing and variable-refresh guidance. **Pre-release, not yet tested on a device.**

## New

- **Presets** (Quick Access → Power, and the Power tab's profile editor): **Battery** (10 W, boost 15 W, power-saving energy preference, CPU boost off, GPU power saving), **Balanced** (17 W / 25 W), **Performance** (28 W with the device's own boost) and **Max** (the highest this power source allows). One tap sets the profile the sliders edit (the game's own one, else Default), with **Undo** for 30 s. Moving one of those sliders by hand makes it **Custom**. A GPU speed cap you set stays. The values are starting points from ONEXPLAYER's own presets and the firmware's values, and the line says **not yet measured on this device** until a measurement exists.
- **Device check** (Power tab): **Check power controls** moves each power setting OXP3 Deck controls by a small step (1 W, 50 MHz, one energy-preference step, ...), reads it back and puts the previous value back at once. It runs only with no game running, stops when a game starts or the charger changes, and always ends by applying your profile again, so nothing stays changed. It lists what works, what the firmware locks and what this device does not support, and shows the read-only **Power time windows** of both power interfaces. **Measure 60 s** records average and peak package power, GPU busy, battery draw and throttle reasons, idle or while a game runs. The results are kept and saved next to the diagnose archive.
- **Save power on low battery** (on by default once power control is on): on battery at 20 % or less (adjustable, 10–40 %), the Default profile runs its **Low battery** values (the Battery preset unless you change them under **Edit values for: Low battery**) until 25 % or until you plug in. It never raises a limit (each value is the lower of the two), and a game with its own profile keeps its values unless **Also for games with their own profile** is on. A **Low battery** line says when it is active or held back.
- **Power tips while playing** (on by default): while a game runs with power control on, OXP3 Deck reads the power use every 5 s from a few cheap files (no GPU clock reads, which would wake the GPU). After 5 minutes of play, **This game** may say, for example, "GPU has headroom: 15 W would likely keep your fps (now 25 W)" or that the game is power-limited. Nothing changes until you tap **Use 15 W** (then **Undo**); **Dismiss** hides that tip.
- **Variable refresh is safe** (Quick Access → Power and the Fixes tab): bundles oxp3-tool 0.4.4, which tells when the kernel has the variable-refresh fix (7.2.10 or later). Details say how to turn VRR on in Steam, which frame limits suit it, and that OLED screens can flicker slightly at low frame rates (not checked on this screen yet). **Don't show again** hides it until the kernel changes.

## Changed

- Less background work in games: with no Quick Access panel open and nothing changed lately, the check that your power limits are still in place runs every 10 s instead of every 2 s while a game runs. It goes back to the usual rate after any change.
