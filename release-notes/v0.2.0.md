Adds power control for the ONEXPLAYER 3 (Panther Lake), replacing SimpleDeckyTDP. **Not yet tested on a real device.**

## Before you update

Uninstall SimpleDeckyTDP: two plugins setting the same limits overwrite each other. OXP3 Deck can import its default and per-game profiles (Power tab).

## What's new

- **TDP:** sustained (PL1) and boost (PL2) limits, written through both of the chip's power-limit interfaces (intel-rapl and intel-rapl-mmio). The chip uses the lower of the two, which is why SimpleDeckyTDP's setting often had no effect. Range 5-35 W on battery and up to 40 W on AC, with an opt-in extended range up to the firmware's 52 W boost limit on AC. The 160 W peak limit is never touched.
- **GPU:** minimum and maximum clock for the Arc B390 (100-2300 MHz) on the xe driver. The media engine is left alone.
- **CPU:** energy-performance preference and turbo, unless power-profiles-daemon or tuned manages them.
- **Profiles:** a default profile, per-game profiles and optional separate AC values, re-applied after sleep, on game change and on plug/unplug.
- **No fighting:** off until you turn it on; detects SimpleDeckyTDP, PowerStation, Handheld Daemon, thermald and others and tells you what to do; stops writing if something else changes the limits.
- Turning TDP control off restores the firmware values; a reboot does too.
