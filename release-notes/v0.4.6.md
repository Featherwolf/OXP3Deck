Louder speakers. **Pre-release, not yet tested on a device.**

- Bundles oxp3-tool 0.4.3 with a new optional fix on the **Fixes** tab: **speaker-profile**. It adds a PipeWire filter, *OXP3 Speakers*, that only the built-in speakers pass through: a high-pass at 180 Hz so the small speakers are not fed bass they cannot play, gentle EQ, +8 dB of gain, and a peak limiter so that gain never clips or overdrives the speakers. Headphones, HDMI and Bluetooth bypass it, and the speaker stays the output Steam's and KDE's volume control.
- Apply and Revert restart PipeWire briefly (sound stops for a moment). After applying, set the speaker volume back to 100 %: above 100 % is software gain that clips, and the status warns about it.
