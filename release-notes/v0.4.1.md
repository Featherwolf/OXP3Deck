Fixes from the first device reports. **Still a pre-release.**

- **Fix for me now holds.** power-profiles-daemon, TuneD, thermald and intel_lpmd can be started again on demand, so stopping and disabling them was not enough (power-profiles-daemon came back and reset the energy preference). Fix for me now also masks them, after saying so in its dialog; Undo unmasks them and restores exactly what was there. A unit an admin already overrode or masked is left alone.
- **CPU controls:** per-core caps no longer fail while CPU boost is off, and status rows say what is actually in effect and why a control is not applied.
- **Start in DirectX 12:** a per-game toggle that adds the game's DX12 switch after `%command%` (Witcher 3: `-dx12`; its XeSS frame generation option only stays on in DX12). Automatic launch options add it for Witcher 3 when frame generation is set; Copy includes it.
- **Frame generation refused:** the Games row reads XeSS frame generation's own log (with `XESS_LOG=1`) and says why it does not start, e.g. "Turn on Xe Low Latency in the game's graphics menu".
- **Launch-option warnings:** Monster Hunter Wilds is marked "frame generation crashes"; `FSR=xess` / `FSR_FG=inject` and forced frame generation are warned against for games with their own XeSS or frame generation, and automatic launch options never add frame generation.
- **Scheduler mode:** a warning that changing it while a game runs can freeze the game, and a confirm before doing so.
- **XeSS tab:** a warning about the known xess-xmx-linux 20261104 driver-store issue with proton-cachyos (fixed upstream, not yet released).
